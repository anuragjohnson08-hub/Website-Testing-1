PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

Formulated for Wellness.

01

BONEBRO

Bone & Nutritional Support

KEY INGREDIENTS Calcium, Vitamin K2-7, Zinc, Magnesium, Vitamin D3

A specialized formula designed to support normal bone health. BONEBRO provides essential calcium nutritional support alongside crucial vitamins and minerals that assist in the body's natural maintenance processes.

02

RED-XT

Iron & Micronutrient Support

KEY INGREDIENTS Ferrous Ascorbate, Folic Acid, Cyanocobalamin, Pyridoxine, Vitamin D3

Comprehensive iron nutrition. RED-XT combines highly absorbable iron forms with folate and Vitamin B12, providing essential nutritional support related to normal red blood cell formation.

CEREBRA-CB

Cognitive Nutritional Support

KEY INGREDIENTS Cerebroprotein, Omega-3, Vitamin E, Vitamin D3, Vitamin B12

Targeted neuro-nutritional support. Formulated with Omega-3 and essential vitamins to support general cognitive function and overall nervous system health as part of a balanced lifestyle.

Protein & Daily Nutrition

Comprehensive daily nutritional support. Protizen-HP provides essential protein nutrition necessary for muscle support and overall recovery, ideal for maintaining an active and healthy life.
